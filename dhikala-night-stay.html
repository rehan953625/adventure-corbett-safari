Inside the Core Tiger Reserve

What Makes Staying at Dhikala Forest Rest House Extraordinary?

Staying overnight inside Dhikala Forest Rest House (FRH) is the ultimate dream of wildlife photographers, naturalists, and avid travelers. Situated deep inside the core tiger reserve, approximately 32 km from Dhangarhi Gate, the historic Dhikala campus sits right at the edge of the sprawling Ramganga river reservoir and savannah grasslands (Chaurs).

Unlike external luxury hotels, Dhikala offers an authentic, raw forest experience protected by a heavy solar-electric perimeter fence. At night, you fall asleep to the roar of Royal Bengal Tigers, the trumpeting of wild elephants, and the alarm calls of spotted deer reverberating across the river valley.

📅 Operating Season Dhikala FRH opens strictly from 15th November to 15th June each year. It remains completely closed during the monsoon season for forest rejuvenation.
🚙 Mandatory 4x4 Gypsy Rule You cannot enter on foot or in personal cars. A registered 4x4 Gypsy with an authorized driver must be booked for your entire stay from Ramnagar.

Living in the Wild

Important Forest Rules, Canteen Meals & Facilities

A forest rest house is not a luxury resort. It is a strictly regulated eco-conservation facility governed by forest department laws.

🍽️ KMVN Dining & Canteen Meals

  • Buffet Dining: Dhikala has a spacious canteen operated by KMVN serving wholesome vegetarian buffet meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner).
  • Strict Prohibition: Non-vegetarian food and alcohol consumption are strictly prohibited by law inside the entire tiger reserve.
  • Tea & Snacks: Morning tea and evening biscuits/snacks are served in the central canteen courtyard.

⚡ Solar Electricity & Connectivity

  • Limited Power: To avoid noise pollution, electricity runs via generator/solar systems primarily from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM.
  • Mobile Network: Almost zero mobile network connectivity inside Dhikala. Disconnect completely and immerse yourself in nature.
  • Charging Points: Charge camera batteries and phones in the evening hours when power is active.

🚧 Gate Entry & Campus Rules

  • Dhangarhi Gate Deadline: Entry closes at 4:00 PM in winter and 4:30 PM in summer. Late arrivals are strictly barred entry.
  • Solar Electric Fence: Walking outside the campus fence on foot is strictly illegal and life-threatening.
  • Strict Silence: Loud music, speakers, and campfires are strictly banned inside the reserve.

Permit Windows

Official Dhikala FRH Permit Rules & Gypsy Coordination (Step-by-Step)

Dhikala has very limited rooms (around 32 rooms in total). Because demand is immense, understanding the official booking cycle is crucial.

1. Official Government System

Night stay permits are released by the Uttarakhand Forest Department on their official portal: corbettgov.org.

2. 45 to 90 Days Advance Window

Indian national quota opens 45 days in advance at 10:00 AM IST. Foreign national quota opens 90 days in advance. Weekend dates sell out in seconds.

3. Complete Identification

Have valid government ID cards (such as Passport or approved identification) ready for all passengers. Once booked, names, dates, and room categories are strictly non-transferable.

4. Mandatory Gypsy Coordination

After your permit is confirmed, coordinate your authorized 4x4 Gypsy and pickup from Ramnagar with our local travel desk.

Essential Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Dhikala Night Stay

Clear facts to help you prepare for a memorable, comfortable stay in the heart of Jim Corbett.

1. Is Dhikala night stay safe for families and children?
Yes, completely safe. The entire residential campus of Dhikala Forest Rest House is fortified by a high-voltage solar electric fence to prevent wild animals from entering the living area. Forest guards patrol the perimeter continuously. However, walking outside the fenced boundary on foot is strictly prohibited.
2. Is the guide fee included in the Dhikala package?
No, the guide fee is NOT included. Uttarakhand Forest Department rules mandate that clients must pay the certified nature guide fee (approx. ₹700 to ₹1,000 per safari shift) directly in cash to the guide allotted for the drive.
3. Can I take my personal car inside Dhikala?
No. Private civilian cars, SUVs, or commercial city cabs are strictly forbidden past Dhangarhi Gate. Only registered, green-emission 4x4 open Gypsies registered with Corbett Tiger Reserve are permitted to enter Dhikala.
4. What should I pack for a Dhikala night stay?
Pack warm layers and windproof jackets (winter mornings on open Gypsies reach 5°C to 8°C), camera gear with extra batteries (power is limited to 4 hours in the evening), personal toiletries, essential medications, cash (cards and online UPI do not work inside due to no network), and valid original government IDs.
5. How many nights can one stay at Dhikala?
To prevent monopolization of limited rooms, the forest department restricts bookings to a maximum of 2 or 3 consecutive nights per permit.
