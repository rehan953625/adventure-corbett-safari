The Golden Gateway to Dhikala

Why Canter Safari is Unique in Jim Corbett

Dhikala is one of the best-known zones of Corbett Tiger Reserve. With its sweeping Savannah grasslands (Chaurs), Ramganga river channels, and backdrop of the Shivalik foothills, it is known for Royal Bengal Tigers, wild elephant herds, gharials, and hog deer.

Because private 4x4 Gypsies are strictly restricted to overnight Forest Rest House (FRH) residents inside Dhikala, the 16-seater open Canter Safari is the exclusive official medium for day tourists staying in external private resorts around Ramnagar to enter and experience the heart of Dhikala.

🚌 Vehicle Type & View 16-seater heavy-duty open safari bus with elevated tiered seating, offering superb vantage points for spotting tigers over tall grassland.
⏱️ Safari Duration Approx. 5 to 5.5 hours (~32 km deep forest drive each way from Dhangarhi Gate to Dhikala Complex).

Daily Schedule & Itinerary

Canter Safari Shift Timings & Scenic Forest Route

Two Canters operate in the morning shift and two in the afternoon shift each day. Timings are regulated strictly according to seasonal daylight hours.

🌅 Morning Shift (Dawn to Noon)

  • Boarding Time: ~5:30 AM to 6:00 AM (from Ramnagar / Dhangarhi)
  • Safari Duration: ~6:00 AM to 11:30 AM (approx. 5 hours)
  • Why Choose Morning: Golden morning light across Dhikala Chaur, active predators on tracks, fresh pugmarks, and cool river breeze along Ramganga.

🌇 Afternoon Shift (Noon to Sunset)

  • Boarding Time: ~11:30 AM to 12:00 PM (from Ramnagar / Dhangarhi)
  • Safari Duration: ~12:00 PM to 5:30 PM (approx. 5 hours)
  • Why Choose Afternoon: Heavy elephant herds crossing the Ramganga riverbed and predators resting near waterholes during warm midday hours.

📍 Boarding Point & Gate Rules

  • Reporting Point: Corbett Tiger Reserve Reception, Ramnagar or Dhangarhi Gate (~18 km from Ramnagar on Ranikhet Road).
  • Strict Reporting: Reach the gate at least 30 to 45 minutes prior to departure for mandatory physical biometric and ID verification.
  • ID Requirement: Original Government ID card mandatory for each passenger.

🗺️ The 32 km Safari Route: Dhangarhi to Dhikala Complex

The Canter Safari starts at Dhangarhi Gate and navigates through the heart of the reserve:

  1. Dhangarhi Gate: Forest verification point, nature shop & trophy museum.
  2. Sultan Forest Rest House (~8 km): Dense Sal canopy with frequent leopard and sambar deer sightings.
  3. Gairal FRH & Crocodile Point (~18 km): River Ramganga hugs the road; spot basking Mugger crocodiles and endangered Gharials.
  4. Champion Road & High Bank: Dramatic vantage points overlooking the river gorge where wild elephants frequently bathe.
  5. Dhikala Chaur & FRH Campus (~31 km): Vast savannah grassland, watchtowers, and prime tiger territory.
