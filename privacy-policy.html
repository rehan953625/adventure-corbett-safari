Welcome to Adventure Corbett Safari (https://adventurecorbettsafari.com). We are an independent private travel and safari tour coordination oprator, Uttarakhand. We respect the privacy of every guest and are committed to safeguarding the personal and identification information you share with us when enquiring about or booking a safari tour, Canter excursion, or resort stay.

1. Information We Collect

To check slot availability, process safari entry permits, and coordinate private 4x4 Gypsy vehicles or resort stays in Corbett Tiger Reserve, we collect only the details strictly required for your booking:

  • Contact Information: Your full name, mobile/WhatsApp number, and email address when you submit an enquiry via WhatsApp, email, or phone.
  • Mandatory Safari Permit Details: Full name, age, gender, nationality, and government photo ID number (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving Licence for Indian nationals; Passport number for Foreign nationals) of all guests entering the reserve, as required by forest entry gate regulations.
  • Travel & Stay Preferences: Preferred safari zone, date, shift (Morning/Evening), vehicle format (Gypsy/Canter), and resort check-in/check-out dates.
  • Basic Technical Data: Standard non-identifying browser analytics (such as device type and pages visited) to maintain website speed and mobile usability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used exclusively for fulfilling the travel services you request from our Ramnagar desk:

  • Checking real-time safari slot availability and submitting permit booking requests on your behalf.
  • Coordinating your registered 4x4 Gypsy vehicle, driver pickup from your resort, and mandatory nature guide at the entry gate.
  • Confirming room reservations with our partner resorts (such as Maya The Forest Resort, Maulik Mansion Resort, Corbett Le Reserve, Taj Corbett Resort & Spa, or Aahana).
  • Sending booking vouchers, gate reporting times, and trip updates via WhatsApp, SMS, or email.

3. Strict Confidentiality of Government ID Proofs

Because Corbett Tiger Reserve entry permits are issued against a traveller's original government ID, we handle ID numbers/documents with strict confidentiality. Your ID details are used only for generating your official safari permit or completing resort check-in formalities. We never sell, rent, trade, or publish your personal identification data or phone number to third-party telemarketers or unauthorized entities.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We share relevant booking details only with the operational entities necessary to execute your itinerary:

  • Forest Reserve Permit System: Guest names, ages, genders, and ID numbers required to generate your entry voucher.
  • Partner Resorts & Registered Gypsy Drivers: Lead guest name, contact number, and pickup location so your driver can coordinate your morning or afternoon transfer.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by applicable Indian law or lawful government authority.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

We retain booking records only for as long as necessary to complete your safari tour, provide customer support, and comply with standard tax/accounting regulations. Once your trip is completed, you may request immediate deletion of your ID copies from our WhatsApp/email records by contacting us at adventuresafari120@gmail.com.

6. Cookies & Third-Party Links

Our website uses minimal functional storage and a privacy-friendly YouTube click-to-play video facade (no third-party tracking cookies are loaded until you explicitly click Play on the field video). External links such as WhatsApp (`wa.me`) operate under their respective platform privacy policies.

7. Contact Our Privacy & Support Desk
