Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how Adventure Corbett Safari collects, uses, and safeguards your contact details strictly for travel communication, local transport coordination, and resort stay enquiries.

1. Introduction & Commitment

At Adventure Corbett Safari (adventurecorbettsafari.com), operated under our GST-registered proprietorship firm Rahino (Proprietor: Mohd Rehan | GSTIN: 05FIKPR6535Q1ZB), we respect your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you visit our website, submit an enquiry form, or contact our travel desk, and how that information is handled with strict confidentiality. We are committed to maintaining the highest ethical standards of transparency, data integrity, and visitor trust across all our trip planning workflows.

2. Information We Collect & Why

Strict Purpose Limitation:

We collect client contact information—specifically your full name, mobile/WhatsApp telephone number, email address, and travel preferences—solely and strictly for the explicit purpose of responding to your enquiries, communicating safari details, sharing customized accommodation quotations, and coordinating ground transfer logistics.

  • Direct Contact Details: Your name, phone/WhatsApp number, and email address provided in website enquiry forms are used solely to answer your questions and share requested safari and hotel quotes.
  • No Government ID Numbers Collected on Website: Our website enquiry forms do not request or collect sensitive government identity numbers (such as Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving Licence copies). Official safari permits are governed by the Uttarakhand Forest Department portal (corbettgov.org), and visitors must carry their original physical photo ID directly to the forest entry gate for verification by forest authorities.
  • Technical Metadata & Analytics: Generic, non-personally identifiable technical diagnostic parameters such as browser types, device screen resolutions, referring domains, and operating systems may be processed in server logs purely to optimize mobile responsiveness and page performance.

3. No Sale, Rental or Unauthorized Sharing of Personal Data

We DO NOT sell, rent, trade, lease, or monetize your personal information to any third-party marketing companies, telemarketers, or advertising networks. Your contact details are stored securely and accessed only by our authorized staff to process your travel coordination. We never share customer lead databases with unaffiliated commercial operators, third-party sales callers, or bulk emailing syndicates.

4. Financial & Payment Card Information

Our website does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card details, CVV codes, net banking credentials, or personal banking passwords. All financial transactions are coordinated via standard bank transfers, official government payment gateways, or verified business UPI channels with proper invoices. You will never be asked to input financial card pins or confidential passwords on this platform.

5. Independent Tour Operator Disclaimer

Adventure Corbett Safari is an independent private tour operator. We are not a government agency, nor are we affiliated with the official administrative department of Corbett Tiger Reserve. Official safari permits and Forest Rest House bookings are governed directly by the official government portal at corbettgov.org under Uttarakhand Forest Department policies.

6. Data Security Measures & Retention

We implement commercially reasonable physical, administrative, and technological safety measures to protect the personal information shared with us. Inquiries submitted via our web forms are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS sockets to prevent unauthorized interception. Customer communication records are archived only for the duration required to complete the safari booking and service fulfillment, after which inactive inquiry threads are periodically purged.
