Jim Corbett Jeep Safari Zones

Each zone offers a different landscape and safari experience. Entry, vehicle access, timings and permits depend on current park rules and availability; confirm details with the authorised booking channel before travelling.

ZONE 01

Bijrani Zone

Bijrani is known for a mix of forest, grassland and open habitat that creates varied scenery during a safari. Visitors may enjoy the changing landscape and opportunities to observe birds and wildlife in their natural surroundings. Sightings are never guaranteed, and entry is subject to permit availability and current operating rules.

ZONE 02

Jhirna Zone

Jhirna is a forest safari area in the Corbett landscape, with dry deciduous vegetation and varied terrain. The drive can appeal to visitors interested in the region’s natural habitats, birdlife and forest atmosphere. Check the current access status, reporting point and permitted safari timings before planning your visit.

ZONE 03

Dhela Zone

Dhela offers a nature-focused safari through forest and open patches in the wider Corbett region. Its landscape can provide opportunities to notice different plants, birds and signs of wildlife along the route. Access and available safari slots can vary by season and official arrangements, so confirm current details before booking.

ZONE 04

Dhikala Zone

Dhikala is widely associated with expansive grasslands, riverine scenery and views across the Corbett landscape. Access arrangements differ from other zones and may involve specific day-visit or accommodation rules. Do not assume that every visitor or vehicle can enter; verify the current official permit type, route and reporting instructions.

ZONE 05

Durga Devi Zone

Durga Devi is known for hilly terrain, forested slopes and river corridors in the northeastern part of the Corbett landscape. The route can be especially interesting for visitors who appreciate varied topography and birdlife. Road conditions, access and safari operation are subject to official rules and seasonal conditions.

ZONE 06

Garjiya Zone

Garjiya is one of the named safari areas associated with the Corbett tourism landscape. Before selecting this zone, confirm whether jeep safaris are operating for your travel date, the authorised entry point and the applicable permit process. Follow your driver and park staff instructions throughout the safari.

ZONE 07

Pakhro Zone

Pakhro is associated with the southern part of the Corbett landscape and has specific access and management arrangements. Safari availability and permitted activities can differ from other tourism zones. Confirm the latest official status before publishing availability or making travel plans for this area.
