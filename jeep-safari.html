Explore Jeep Safari

A jeep safari offers a small-group way to explore designated safari routes with an authorised driver. It can suit families or friends who prefer a private vehicle experience. Routes, entry permits and timings depend on official availability, and wildlife sightings cannot be guaranteed.

Confirm the permitted zone, reporting point, departure time, permit details and what is included before making a booking. The guide fee is payable by the guest separately, as specified for this draft rate. All prices, fees and availability should be reconfirmed before travel.
