2. Official Safari & Forest Rest House Permit Rules

Important Clarification Regarding Official Permits:

Adventure Corbett Safari does not generate, sell, or independently issue government safari permits. Official safari entry permits and Forest Rest House (FRH) night stay reservations are exclusively governed, controlled, and issued by the Uttarakhand Forest Department through their centralized portal at corbettgov.org.

Visitors should always verify official rules, permit tariffs, and live availability directly on the government portal. Our charges apply strictly to private vehicle rental, local resort pickup/drop transfers, and trip planning coordination.

  • Non-Refundable & Non-Transferable: Under strict Forest Department rules, once a safari permit is generated on the government portal, it cannot be refunded, transferred, exchanged, or rescheduled under any circumstances.
  • Mandatory Original ID: All visitors must carry the exact original physical government photo ID (such as Passport, Voter ID, or approved official identity documentation) used during permit booking. Mismatches will lead to denied entry by forest authorities at the gate without refund.
  • Zone Allotment: Safari zones are allotted on a first-come, first-served basis according to official quotas. We cannot alter or swap zone allocations once approved by the government system.

3. Mandatory Nature Guide Fee (Direct Client Payment)

Guide Fee is NOT Included in Any Tour Package:

In accordance with official Uttarakhand Forest Department rules, hiring an authorized registered nature guide is compulsory for every safari entry into Corbett Tiger Reserve. The Guide Fee (approx. ₹1,000 per safari shift) is strictly EXCLUDED from all vehicle, safari, or stay packages.

The guide fee must be paid directly by the client in cash to the assigned forest guide at the gate entry counter before starting the safari.

4. Wildlife Sightings & Natural Conditions Disclaimer

Jim Corbett National Park is a protected natural wild habitat and not an artificial zoo or enclosure. Wildlife movements (including tigers, leopards, and elephants) are 100% natural and cannot be scheduled, guaranteed, or manipulated. We make zero claims or guarantees of tiger or predator sightings.

5. Forest Closures, Weather & Force Majeure

The park director and forest department hold unilateral authority to shut down gates, suspend safaris, alter routes, or close zones due to heavy monsoon rains, flooded river crossings, VIP security movements, or wildlife protection emergencies. Adventure Corbett Safari shall not be held liable for compensatory damages or refunds for government-mandated closures beyond our operational control.

6. Safari Vehicle & Passenger Capacity

Each 4x4 Gypsy is authorized to carry a maximum of 6 adult visitors + 2 children under 5 years, plus the driver and guide. Overloading is strictly prohibited by park rules. Visitors must arrive at the resort lobby on time for pickup; delays resulting in gate closure are solely the responsibility of the client.

7. Refund, Modification & Cancellation Policy

Private resort accommodations and local transport arrangements coordinated via our travel desk are governed by transparent modification and cancellation rules:

  • Private Gypsy / Local Transfer Cancellations: Cancellations requested 7 or more days prior to the scheduled trip date are eligible for a refund of private vehicle/transfer coordination charges (after deducting standard payment processing/administrative costs). Cancellations within 7 days of travel or no-shows at the pickup point are non-refundable.
  • Private Resort & Hotel Stays: Resort bookings are governed by the respective property's cancellation schedule. Peak-season reservations (including Diwali, Christmas, New Year, and long national holidays) are non-refundable once confirmed.
  • Official Forest Permits: As governed by the Uttarakhand Forest Department (corbettgov.org), official entry permits and Forest Rest House bookings are strictly non-refundable, non-transferable, and cannot be rescheduled once issued.
  • Refund Processing Timeline: Any approved refunds for eligible private resort or transport cancellations are processed back to the original payment source within 5 to 7 working days.
