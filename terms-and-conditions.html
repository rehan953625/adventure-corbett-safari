2. Strict Non-Refundable & Non-Transferable Permit Policy

All travellers must note the following forest reserve regulations before confirming a booking:

  • No Cancellations or Refunds on Issued Permits: Once a Jeep Safari, Canter Safari, or Core Lodge entry permit is generated in the system against your government ID, it is 100% non-refundable, non-cancellable, and non-transferable.
  • No Date, Shift, or Zone Modifications: Issued permits cannot be rescheduled to another date, shift (Morning/Evening), or safari zone under any circumstances.
  • If Quota Is Full Prior to Permit Issuance: If a specific zone slot becomes unavailable before your permit is generated, our desk will offer you an alternative open zone (such as Phato, Garjiya, Jhirna, Dhela, or Sitabani) or refund the unissued permit amount.

3. Mandatory Government ID & Gate Verification Rules

Entry into Corbett Tiger Reserve is strictly regulated at every gate:

  • Every visitor must carry the exact same original physical government photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving Licence, or Passport for foreign nationals) whose number was submitted at the time of booking.
  • If a guest fails to produce their original ID at the gate or provides incorrect name/ID details during booking, forest officials may deny entry without any refund.
  • A maximum of 6 adults (plus up to 2 children below 5 years, subject to gate rules) are allowed in a single 4x4 Gypsy alongside the driver and mandatory forest guide.

4. Reporting Time & Park Safety Conduct

Guests must be ready at their resort pickup point at least 30 minutes prior to the scheduled safari shift (typically 5:30 AM for Morning Safari and 1:30 PM for Evening Safari). Late arrival at the entry gate may result in shortened drive time or gate closure, for which the oprator cannot be held liable. Inside the forest, visitors must strictly follow the instructions of the forest guide and driver, remain inside the vehicle at all times, wear muted/earth-toned clothing, and refrain from littering, smoking, flash photography, or playing loud music.

5. Natural Wildlife Sightings & Weather Closures

  • No Sighting Guarantee: Corbett Tiger Reserve is an unfenced natural forest spanning 1,288 sq. km. Wildlife movement is completely natural—no honest oprator, driver, or guide can guarantee a tiger sighting on any drive.
  • Weather or Administrative Gate Closures: The Forest Department reserves the right to restrict or close any safari zone at short notice due to heavy rainfall, river flooding, VIP movement, or administrative orders. Any refund or adjustment in such force majeure situations is governed strictly by the official forest department refund decision.

6. Resort Booking & Cancellation Terms

For guests booking resort packages (such as Maya The Forest Resort, Maulik Mansion Resort, Corbett Le Reserve, Taj Corbett Resort & Spa, or Aahana) through our team, standard check-in is at 1:00 PM / 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Resort room modifications or cancellations are subject to the respective hotel's seasonal cancellation policy shared in your booking voucher.
