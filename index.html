WELCOME TO THE CORBETT REGION

Welcome to Jim Corbett

Jim Corbett National Park, in Uttarakhand, is one of India’s best-known natural destinations. Its varied landscapes include sal forests, grasslands, river corridors and the foothills of the Himalayas. These habitats support a rich range of wildlife and birdlife, making the wider Corbett region appealing to nature lovers, photographers and travellers who want to experience the outdoors.

A visit to Corbett can be more than a single activity. Depending on the season, availability and your interests, you may plan a permitted forest safari, explore nearby villages and riverside landscapes, enjoy local food, or choose accommodation around the park landscape. Each experience offers a different way to appreciate the region, from early-morning forest atmosphere to relaxed evenings after a day outdoors.

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed: animals move freely in their natural habitat, and every safari is different. A responsible visit means following park rules, listening to authorised staff, keeping a respectful distance from wildlife, avoiding litter and keeping noise to a minimum. Safari zones, timings, permits and access rules can change, so confirm current details with the relevant official authority before travelling.

Adventure Corbett Safari helps visitors compare experience options and enquire about their plans. Tell us your travel dates, group size and preferred activity so you can check the details that matter before confirming. Prices shown on this page are indicative rates supplied for this draft and should be reconfirmed, including taxes, permit charges, availability and additional guide or operator fees.

PRIVATE VEHICLE

Jeep Safari

A jeep safari offers a small-group way to explore designated safari routes with an authorised driver. It can suit families or friends who prefer a private vehicle experience. Routes, entry permits and timings depend on official availability, and wildlife sightings cannot be guaranteed. Confirm what the quoted amount includes before booking.

Indicative price₹7,500 per jeep

Guide fee is payable by the guest separately. Confirm permit fees, taxes and inclusions before booking.

Canter Safari

A canter safari is a shared, larger-vehicle experience that may suit visitors travelling in a group. Verify current official rules, seat availability and whether the listed rate applies to Indian or international visitors before making plans.
