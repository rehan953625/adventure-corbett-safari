Who We Are

Your Independent Local Wildlife Travel Partners

At Adventure Corbett Safari (adventurecorbettsafari.com), we are an independent travel coordination desk operating from the immediate foothills of Nainital and Udham Singh Nagar districts in Uttarakhand. Having grown up in close proximity to the reserve, our team understands the pulse, weather, river corridors, and seasonal shifts of Corbett Tiger Reserve like second nature.

Unlike large metropolitan aggregator portals that treat safari permits as transactional tickets, we prioritize genuine guidance. We inform our travellers in advance about real wildlife sighting behaviors, gate distances from hotels, winter morning preparation, and strict forest ethics.

📍 Genuine Local Ground Presence Based locally near Ramnagar and Kashipur. Our drivers, registered Gypsies, and resort connections .
🛡️ Transparent & Ethical Operation We never make false claims of 100% tiger sightings. We respect the forest rules and ensure 100% compliance with Uttarakhand Forest Department regulations.
