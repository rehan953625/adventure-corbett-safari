OUR APPROACH

Helping You Plan Your Visit

Adventure Corbett Safari is a visitor information and enquiry website focused on safari experiences and stay options in the Corbett region. We aim to make it easier for travellers to compare experiences, understand the details to check and send an enquiry about their plans.

Wildlife is unpredictable, and all visits should respect official park rules, authorised operators, local communities and animal welfare. Before making payment, visitors should confirm permits, rates, inclusions, cancellation terms and availability with the relevant authorised provider.
