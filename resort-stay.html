Luxury, Relaxation & Nature

Why Stay in a Private Resort Around Jim Corbett?

While Forest Rest Houses inside the reserve offer basic, rustic living with limited solar power, private resorts situated around Ramnagar and the Kosi River belt provide the perfect balance of wildlife adventure and modern holiday luxury.

Here, you can unwind in air-conditioned riverfront cottages, plunge into crystal-clear swimming pools, indulge in multi-cuisine buffet dining (including non-vegetarian delicacies prohibited inside core forest rest houses), enjoy evening bonfires with live acoustic music, and stay seamlessly connected with high-speed Wi-Fi.

🚙 Doorstep Safari Pickup Your authorized 4x4 Gypsy picks your group up right from the resort lobby 30–45 minutes prior to gate opening time.
🏊 Amenities & Entertainment Swimming pools, spas, expansive lawns, indoor/outdoor games, conference halls, and curated kids play zones.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Jim Corbett Resorts

Everything you need to know about resort selection, meal plans, safari pickup, and group bookings.

1. Is non-vegetarian food and alcohol allowed in private resorts in Jim Corbett?
Yes. While non-vegetarian food and alcohol are strictly banned inside government Forest Rest Houses (like Dhikala and Bijrani FRH), private resorts in Ramnagar, Dhikuli, Kyari, and Marchula serve delicious non-vegetarian buffet dishes and have bar licenses or permit guests to enjoy drinks inside designated areas.
2. Do resorts provide pickup for morning and evening safaris?
Yes. When you book an authorized 4x4 Gypsy safari through our travel desk, your private Gypsy arrives directly at your resort reception in Ramnagar or Dhikuli, drives you to the designated safari gate (e.g. Bijrani, Jhirna, Dhela, Garjiya), and drops you safely back at your resort after the drive.
3. Is the forest guide fee included in the resort safari combo?
No, the guide fee is NOT included. Under Corbett Tiger Reserve regulations, a registered forest guide is assigned via roster at the entry gate. Clients must pay the guide fee (approx. ₹700 to ₹1,000 per safari shift) directly in cash to the guide at the gate.
4. What is the best meal plan to book for a Corbett resort?
We strongly recommend booking the MAP Plan (Modified American Plan - Breakfast + Dinner). Since you will likely be on a safari or exploring local attractions (such as Corbett Waterfall or Garjiya Devi Temple) during lunchtime, having breakfast and dinner included at the resort offers the greatest flexibility and value.
5. Can we host destination weddings or corporate offsites in Corbett?
Yes, Corbett is one of North India’s top destinations for weddings and corporate conferences. Several luxury properties in Dhikuli and Ramnagar offer 200+ room capacities, massive banquet halls, manicured riverfront lawns, and audio-visual setups for 500+ guests. Contact our desk for group rates.
